I was recently invited to give a talk about Methodist history and racial justice to the William and Mary Wesley Foundation. While the first half focused on standard denominational history, the second half I focused in on lessons we can draw from the Student Christian Movement, using my [_Modern American History_](https://doi.org/10.1017/mah.2022.2) article. Overall, the experience was a lot of fun, and I'm thankful to Rev. Ryan LaRock for inviting me!
